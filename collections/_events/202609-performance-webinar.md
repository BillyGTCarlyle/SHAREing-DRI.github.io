## Summary
The Durham team working on SHAREing will share updates on the performance assessment methodology they have been developing over the past year. The webinar will introduce the approach, discuss the progress made so far, and provide an opportunity to learn more about how SHAREing is developing ways to assess performance across HPC and research computing environments.
This is a great opportunity to hear directly from the team and find out more about the work taking place within SHAREing’s Performance Assessment Work Package.
